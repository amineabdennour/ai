## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (user-confirmed). One self-contained landing page in `roborder-lp/`.

## Users

Cash-on-delivery (COD) e-commerce sellers who sell through Facebook and Instagram, using both paid ads and organic posts. Their buyers respond publicly in the comments with short purchase signals like "prix?", "how much?" or "I want one". The seller's job is to turn those comments into confirmed orders before the buyer cools off. (Inferred, not confirmed: the comment examples suggest French-speaking and multilingual markets, such as North Africa and the Middle East.)

## Product Purpose

Roborder is a domain-specific AI automation product (roborder.ai). It detects buying-intent comments on Facebook/Instagram ads and posts and automatically moves the conversation into DMs. It also collects leads, capturing purchase intent from comments and DMs so the seller can confirm the order.

Success means more comments turn into confirmed COD orders, fewer leads go cold, and prices stop being exposed in public replies.

## Positioning

The order isn't real until it's confirmed. Roborder covers the gap between a public "prix?" comment and a confirmed COD order. It replies instantly, takes the price conversation private so competitors can't copy it, and leaves the seller with a list of ready-to-confirm leads.

## Operating Context

- Buyers comment on Facebook/Instagram ads and organic posts.
- Today sellers reply by hand. That's slow, leads go cold, and public price replies get copied by competitors.
- COD flow: comment → DM → lead captured → seller confirms order (call/message) → delivery and cash collection.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed:
- Detects purchase-intent comments ("prix?", "how much?", "I want one", and similar) on FB/IG ads and posts.
- Automatically moves the conversation into DMs.
- Leads Collection: captures purchase intent from comments and DMs for seller confirmation.

Unconfirmed (must not be claimed on the page until confirmed): pricing and plan names, free trial length, supported languages, other channels (WhatsApp, TikTok), integrations (Sheets, Shopify, YouCan), stock sync, moderation.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. There are no testimonials, customer counts, logos, metrics or benchmarks. Future work must not fabricate them. Illustrative demo comments/DMs are allowed if they're clearly product demonstrations, not customer claims.

## Product Principles

1. Confirmation is the outcome. Every message ladders to confirmed COD orders, not "engagement".
2. Speed beats intent decay. The first reply wins the buyer.
3. Price talk is private. Public price replies are a gift to competitors.
4. Show the mechanism, don't claim the numbers.
