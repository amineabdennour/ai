# Surface: Roborder landing page (roborder-lp/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: COD sellers who run FB/IG ads and posts. Action: "Start now" → https://roborder.ai (placeholder until the real signup URL arrives). Proof: none real yet. The demo comments, tickets and DMs are illustrative, labelled as demo. There are no invented metrics, testimonials, pricing, or unconfirmed features.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A comment is just noise until it becomes an order ticket. The page prints "prix?" comments as COD order tickets that slide to the DM and get stamped CONFIRMÉ. It refuses the split hero with a dashboard mockup and the three-feature-card row.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is saturated cobalt, the colour of the counter. White thermal-paper tickets with torn, zig-zag edges are the major surfaces. Ticket text is in receipt-printer mono. Headlines use a heavy grotesk. Confirmations are magenta stamp ink at a slight rotation. Dashed perforation rules divide the sections. Raise from the triplicate order book: each section is one numbered "copy" of the order (1/3, 2/3, 3/3), so the structure is disciplined rather than decorative.

STORY: Comments go cold and prices get copied. Roborder replies instantly and moves the price talk to DMs. Leads arrive ready to confirm, so you confirm more COD orders. Start now.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the left (about 55%) is the headline "Every 'prix?' becomes an order." with one line of subcopy and the Start now button, which carries a tear-off stub. On the right, a live comment rail feeds a ticket printer: comments arrive, a ticket prints, and the CONFIRMÉ stamp lands. The top of the problem section shows below the fold.

FORM: Thermal order ticket, candidate 3 of 7. Signature interaction: the printing ticket loop, plus a stamp that lands on scroll. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
